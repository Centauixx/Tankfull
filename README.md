# Tankful

> ⚠️ Work in progress — design exploration, 
> actively being developed.

A vehicle dashboard app that puts your entire 
ownership history in one place — fuel logs, 
mileage tracking, and service reminders.

## What it does

Tankful lets vehicle owners log every fuel fill-up, 
track mileage over time, and get reminded before 
their next service is due. Everything in one clean 
dashboard — no more scattered notes, no more 
guessing when you last got an oil change.

## The problem it solves

Vehicle ownership is full of information you 
need but never have when you need it.

When was the last service? What's my average 
fuel efficiency? How much am I spending per km? 

Most people track nothing. Some use notes apps. 
Nobody has a single place that does all three 
cleanly. Tankful fixes that.

## Core features

- **Fuel logging** — log every fill-up with cost, 
  quantity, and odometer reading
- **Mileage tracking** — automatic km/l calculation 
  and efficiency trends over time
- **Service reminders** — set reminders for your 
  next scheduled service, never miss one again
- **Dashboard** — live stats showing total km, 
  days until next service, cost per km, and 
  average daily distance

## How it was built

Designed as a complete UI/UX exploration with 
a focus on data-dense dashboard design — making 
complex vehicle data feel simple and readable 
at a glance.

- **Design** — UI designed in Google Stitch 
  with a dark high-contrast aesthetic, using 
  gauge-style visualisations for key metrics
- **Status** — Design complete, development 
  in progress

## Design decisions

The dark theme was intentional — vehicle dashboards 
live in low-light environments. The gauge UI mirrors 
the physical instrument cluster language drivers 
already understand. Data clarity over decoration.
